Pathfinder is based on the RIASEC career interest model, designed by psychologist John Holland. Career professionals, educators, and organisations around the world use the RIASEC framework to help identify the types of work and environments that best align with an individual's interests and preferences. It is one of the most widely recognised approaches to career exploration.
Built on the simple principle that people tend to feel more fulfilled in work environments that match their natural strengths, interests and personality, the RIASEC test focuses on which career paths are more likely to be rewarding in the long term.
Rather than measuring your abilities or predicting a single "perfect" career, Pathfinder explores the activities and work environments you're naturally drawn to. Your responses are used to build a personalised profile based on six career interest areas: Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, and Conventional (RIASEC). The assessment is an efficient tool that can help individuals choose a degree or career direction and understand when it might be time for a career pivot.
Your results are designed to support career exploration and self-discovery. While no assessment can define your future, Pathfinder provides a valuable starting point for understanding your unique strengths, motivations, and interests, helping you make more informed decisions about your education, career, and professional development.
